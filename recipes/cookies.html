Cookies

Description

Cookies or known in the US as Chocolate chip cookies. Are a nice treat and fun to bake with a super simple recipe.

Ingredients

  • 120g of butter(best leave it at room temp to soften)
  • 75g of light brown sugar
  • 75g of golden caster sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp of vanilla extract
  • 180g of plain flour
  • 1/2 of bicarbonate of soda
  • 75g of dark chocolate drops
  • 75g of white chocolate drops

Step

  1. Heat oven and line two trays with baking paper.
    • Normal Oven: 180C
    • Fan Oven: 160C
    • Gas Oven: Mark 4
  2. Cream the butter and the two sugars together. Until it is light and fluffy.
  3. Beat in the egg and vanilla to the mix.
  4. Stir in the flour, bicarbonate, both chocolate chips and a pinch os salt.
  5. From this 10 large tbsps of the dough should be able to be made and placed onto the baking paper. Note: Make each one a ball and leave enough space between each one for the dough to spread.
  6. Bake for 10 mintues or until they are firm at the rim but soft in the middle. Cookies will harden when cooled so do not wait for them to be hard in the oven. Otherwise, they will be more like biscuits
  7. Leave them to cool a few mintues and enjoy them warm or let them cool completely. These will keep well for 3 days in an airtight container.
