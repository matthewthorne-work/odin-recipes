Pancakes

Description

Pancakes are known all across the Western world. May different versions are known from the typical flat eurpeon sytle to the fluffy american ones. This will teach you my favourite style of pancakes.

Ingredients

  • 200g of self-rising flour
  • 1½ tsp of baking powder
  • 1 tbsp of caster sugar
  • 3 large eggs
  • 25g of melt butter
  • 200ml of milk
  • Oil for cooking (I like to use Sunflower Oil)
  • Toppings of your choosing

Steps

  1. Mix together the flour, sugar and, baking powder into a large bowl. Add a pinch of salt.
  2. Crack in the 3 large eggs into the mixture and mix well
  3. Add in the butter and keep on mixing
  4. Add in the milk and using a hand whisk or elecetric beater, whisk until smooth
  5. Heat a little bit of butter and oil in a large non-stick pan over a medium heat(Finding the right heat will take a time)
  6. Pour in the batter after the butter starts to froth. Ideally, the pancakes should be about 8cm wide.
  7. Cook the pancakes on mone side until little air bubbles start to form on the top side. Then flip using a spatula
  8. After another minutine or so remove the pancake and make sure it is cooked to how you like them. Do not be afraid to play with the heat or the time it cooks
    9. Keep going until all the batter is used up. Serve as soon as possible with toppings that you like(I like to use golden syrup).
